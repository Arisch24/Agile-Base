# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
1. **Site builders**: freelancers and agencies who set the theme up for clients, rebrand it for each one, and need it fast, stable across updates, and clean.
2. **Site owners**: business owners and DIYers who build their own site in the Site Editor without code and want a professional result out of the box.
3. **Their visitors**: a business's prospective customers, on phones as much as desktops.

## Product Purpose
Agile Base is a WordPress block theme (published on WordPress.org as `agile-base`). Its job is to let someone launch a credible, fast business site from ready-made sections, pages and starter homepages, and rebrand it in minutes, without a page builder. Success: a builder rebrands it for a client in minutes, and an owner gets a professional site with no code.

## Positioning
A fast, neutral base for business and client sites built in the Site Editor: rebrandable through Styles (color and font presets), stable across updates, accessibility-ready, with self-hosted fonts and no theme JavaScript. Internally: what GeneratePress is for classic themes. Never name it in public copy.

## Operating Context
A no-build WordPress block theme: `theme.json`, block templates, template parts and pattern files, edited in the Site Editor. Sites are rebranded by changing colors and fonts in Styles. Content varies widely: long business names, no logo, no images, one post or hundreds, right-to-left languages.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- WordPress.org theme guidelines: GPL, credits, no plugin territory, no external requests, everything translatable.
- WCAG 2.2 AA and the accessibility-ready requirements (spec §7.5).
- Everything editable as core blocks; no custom blocks, shortcodes or required plugins.
- The rebrand model (spec §4): the design must keep its quality with any client's colors, fonts and logo.
- The 1.x compatibility contract and the 2.x API (spec §3).
- Build path: code-first (no image generation in this session).

## Brand Commitments
The name "Agile Base". An unused logo mark exists in `assets/images/`. The 1.x "Velocity" look is the anti-reference. Copy policy (spec D4): placeholder copy for a generic service business that names its slot; never marketing for the theme.

## Evidence on Hand
No real testimonials, customers, benchmarks or photography; none may be fabricated. Image slots stay empty. 1.x screenshots in `build/checks/v1-*` show the current look.

## Product Principles
1. Rebrandable first: every visual value is a token a builder can change in Styles.
2. Fast and quiet: no theme JavaScript, no external requests, small CSS and fonts.
3. Stable: slugs, names, tokens and classes are public API.
4. Editable without code: core blocks only.
5. Neutral by design: placeholder copy serves the client's business, never the theme.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA in every style variation; underlined text links, visible 2px focus outlines, AA contrast in every color preset; right-to-left support.
