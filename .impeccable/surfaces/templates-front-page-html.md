# Front page (Agency homepage)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: a service business's prospective customers; builders rebrand it per client. Action: book a call. Proof: placeholder copy only, no invented claims.

Raises (from declined challengers): one strict spacing unit (Teletext); buttons that invert to solid on hover and focus (HyperCard); numbering as the only ornament (Saville).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Quote Book: every section reads like a numbered, ruled, itemised quote. Refuses the centered hero, soft cards and eyebrow labels.
OWN-WORLD: Canary field, ink text, red serial numerals, hairline rules, boxed buttons. Character from rules, numbering and proportion, never from a hue: every color is a swappable preset.
STORY: A visitor sees what the business does, itemised, and books a call.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Canary field; headline across eight of twelve columns, left aligned, large; serial numeral in the margin (black on a color field, red on paper); one sentence, then a boxed primary button on a ruled line; logo strip as a ruled row.
FORM: Tradesperson's duplicate quote book, candidate 7 of 7, seed key a3572daa.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
