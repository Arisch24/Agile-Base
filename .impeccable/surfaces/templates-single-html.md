# Single post

Mode: Read, inside the Quote Book world (see templates/front-page.html). Job: the reader finishes the article comfortably.

- Header: title, date, author (Avatar + Post Author Name), tags as pills. Tolerates very long titles and unbroken words.
- Measure about 65-70 characters; calm rhythm for headings, lists, quotes, code, tables, images. Wide tables and long code lines scroll inside their own box at 375px, also right to left.
- The Quote Book appears lightly: hairline rules and numerals, never decorating the body text.
- Comments and comment form: hairline-ruled, fields styled with the form tokens.
- Previous/next: two boxed links that invert to solid on hover and focus.
- Core blocks only; links in text underlined; visible 2px focus.
